MedTech & Diagnostics
Technology Assessment Tool

A structured framework for classifying MedTech and diagnostics technologies under development, mapping their development pathway and assessing Technology Readiness Level (TRL).

How to use: Start with the decision tree to identify the technology category, then use the TRL roadmap to understand the development pathway, and the TRL checklist to assess current stage and priority next steps.
